What we record

  • One line per check: which CAPTCHA, the vendor's verdict, score and error codes, how old the token was, how long it took, and whether it asked you to interact.
  • Your browser's user agent, the signals in the table above, and the country Cloudflare reports. No IP address, and no cookie of ours. Google and Cloudflare set their own cookies inside their widgets.
  • Why: to measure, over time and in the open, how CAPTCHAs judge browsers and AI agents. Nothing here is sold, and we run no CAPTCHA-solving service.
